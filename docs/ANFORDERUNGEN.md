# Anforderungen PreZero Challenge

Quelle: `20261113_Readme Challenge PZ.docx`

## Problemstellung

> „Currently, waste containers are typically emptied area by area on a fixed schedule, regardless of how full each container actually is. In many cases, fill-level data is neither collected nor tracked."

> „Your challenge is to analyze unstructured fill-level data, identify key patterns, and build an optimal routing model driven by dynamic operational variables."

## Pflicht-Deliverables

> „By the end of the hackathon, your team's solution should ideally consist of:
> 1. Data Ingestion Tool: A functional interface to upload and process fill-level datasets.
> 2. Pattern Visualization: Visual dashboards illustrating trends, anomalies, and insights.
> 3. Route Engine: A working prototype that generates optimized route proposals.
> 4. Documentation: A brief user guide detailing the technical architecture and approach."

## Analyse-Fragen

> „Pattern Identification: What distinct accumulation patterns or cycles emerge over time?"
> „Container Grouping: Do specific containers share similar behavioral trends or fill rates?"
> „Anomalies & External Factors: How do events like school vacations, public holidays (e.g., Christmas, Easter, Pentecost), or seasonal shifts impact waste volume?"

## Scope-Freigabe

> „We understand the scope and time limits of a hackathon. Our main goal is to see your creative problem-solving approach and prototype concept."

## Unser Scope

- Ein Fahrzeug, Heuristik statt exaktem VRP.
- Luftlinie/Haversine für Distanzen (Platzhalter-Koordinaten).
- Keine Depot-/Shift-Optimierung.
- Keine Gewichts-/Dichte-Modellierung (keine Daten).
- Statische Visualisierungen reichen für den Prototypen.
